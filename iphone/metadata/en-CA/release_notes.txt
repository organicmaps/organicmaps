### Map & places

• Updated OpenStreetMap data as of August 26, 2026
• Updated Wikipedia articles in Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Marathi, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Tamil, Telugu, Turkish, and Urdu
• Added bird hides, stages, hills, and natural arches to the map, plus a new icon for rocks
• Resized peaks, caves, and mountain passes icons
• Protected areas, national parks, and wetlands are now displayed in lighter shades
• Fixed many issues that caused opening hours to be displayed incorrectly
• Reorganized settings into General, Map, Navigation, Network, and Privacy sections
• Removed the compass calibration setting because calibration is now handled automatically
• Fixed compass issues that could cause the current-position arrow to point in the wrong direction or freeze
• The corresponding point is now shown on the map while you drag across the elevation profile in a walking or cycling route preview
• Fixed an app freeze and an issue that occasionally caused taps and gestures to be ignored

### Sharing, bookmarks & tracks

• NEW: Added multi-selection to the Bookmarks and Tracks list, with batch actions to delete, move, and change the colour of selected items, plus Select All and Deselect All
• Added AirDrop support for sharing places
• Sharing a place, bookmark, or current position now sends a readable link with useful information
• Exported bookmarks now preserve their original names and descriptions
• Shared bookmark lists now use their displayed names instead of internal file names
• Fixed an elevation chart display issue that could occur immediately after starting a track recording
• Restored the custom colour picker in the colour palette popover
• Fixed the rendering of HTML descriptions when a bookmark is selected on the map

### Routing & navigation

• NEW: Added a CarPlay dashboard and fixed multiple CarPlay issues
• Fixed the activation of the car navigation map style
• Tapping a route stop or mark now opens it correctly instead of switching between planned routes
• Public transport routes are now planned faster
• Fixed incorrect turn instructions
• Fixed an incorrect start point after a route was rebuilt
• Laterite roads are now treated as having a poor-quality unpaved surface during route planning
• Roads tagged `access=unknown` are now handled like roads with private or destination-only access

### OpenStreetMap editor

• Fixed a crash when an OpenStreetMap upload finished in the background
• Clearing the opening hours field no longer leaves the Save or Done button disabled
• Uploads that partially fail are now retried instead of being reported as fully successful

### Translations

• Added voice guidance in Armenian and Lao
• Updated Help/Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ), added Japanese translation
• Corrected the Japanese translation of "Closed now"
• Improved the Chinese translations of "track" and "disable"
