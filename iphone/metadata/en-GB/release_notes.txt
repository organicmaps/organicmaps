## Routing
• Improved alternative routes and their estimated arrival times
• Added buttons for adding or replacing a stop in a planned route
• Added a setting to optimise the order of intermediate route stops
• The selected alternative route is now preserved when navigation recalculates the route
• The order of route stops is now restored after restarting the app
