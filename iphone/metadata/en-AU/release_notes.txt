## Map & terrain
• NEW: 3D terrain and ocean depths, detailed contour lines in metres and feet, and elevation at any point on the map
• OpenStreetMap data as of 10 November, Wikipedia articles as of 1 November
• Dashed intermittent waterways, clearer water labels and new railway bridge outlines
• Added barbers, Pilates studios, window installers, waterworks and covered reservoirs
• Fixed non-working transliteration and rendering of Hebrew and Arabic map labels
• Reduced memory use and fixed crashes when switching map styles

## Routing & navigation
• Updated route planning and added a button to reverse a planned route, including all intermediate stops
• Driving, cycling and walking now have separate avoidance settings
• Remove the start or destination directly from the route planner
• Add a new destination even when the previous route could not be calculated
• Improved route restoration, recalculation, alternative routes and duration estimates
• Improved public transport arrival estimates, walking connections and station entrance markers
• Fixed saved routes losing their starting segment or using an outdated current-position point
• Fixed outdated speed-camera warnings
• Fixed replacing route points at the stop limit and the clipped routing-settings indicator

## Bookmarks, tracks & areas
• NEW: Import and display areas from KML, KMZ and GeoJSON files, including fills, outlines and holes
• Added track direction arrows and a contrasting outline for selected tracks
• Opening a track from a list now selects its beginning
• Track recordings now keep a backup updated once a minute for recovery after a crash
• KML files preserve a saved route’s intermediate stops and travel mode
• Bookmarks and tracks sort by name in the order expected by your language
• Bookmark list names, descriptions and images refresh correctly after reloading
• Fixed elevation profiles after returning to the map and when viewing flat or zoomed sections
• Fixed crashes when deleting edited tracks and failures on the first bookmark import
• Improved KML, GPX and GeoJSON imports

## CarPlay
• Bookmark lists refresh after edits, reloads and deletions
• Fixed missing bookmarks at the list limit and crashes when selecting deleted entries
