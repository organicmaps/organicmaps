NoGPS Maps is offline navigation for drivers that keeps guiding the car when GPS is unavailable or shows a wrong place.

<b>How it works</b>
• GPS positions are checked against the cell tower and Wi-Fi position and against how far the car could drive. Wrong positions are ignored.
• When GPS is lost, the manual mode turns on: tap the map to place the car, it snaps to the road.
• With an OBD-II ELM327 adapter (Bluetooth) or an ESP32 sensor box (Bluetooth) the app drives the car by itself: turns from the gyroscope, distance from the car speed.
• The car stays on the roads: it snaps to the road, and a turn is moved to the crossing where it happened.
• Speedometer and gyroscope corrections are measured automatically while GPS works.
• The app returns to GPS by itself as soon as it is reliable again.

<b>Also</b>
• Offline OpenStreetMap maps, search, car routes with voice guidance, bookmarks.
• No ads and no tracking: your location never leaves the phone.

<b>Safety</b>
Dead reckoning accumulates errors. Check the road and the signs, keep your attention on driving.
