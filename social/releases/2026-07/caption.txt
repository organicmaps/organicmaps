The July update is out — this month we went bug hunting across public transport, routing, search and bookmarks.

• Route warnings — tolls, ferries, unpaved roads, steps — now show up on alternate routes too
• Notes you report to OpenStreetMap land on the exact spot you picked
• Stop and station names get a white outline, readable in both light and dark
• iOS: a new bookmark color palette with predefined and fully custom colors
• iOS: an Edit button for removing bookmarks, and lists that save themselves
• Android: opening hours with split shifts, a cleaner search bar, a reworked bookmark editor
• OpenStreetMap data updated as of July 14

Get it at get.omaps.org — free, offline, ad-free and open source.
