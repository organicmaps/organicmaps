# Changelog → slide copy

The changelog is written for people who already use the app and read release
notes. A carousel is read by people scrolling past. Rewrite, do not excerpt.

## Picking what goes on a slide

1. Anything with a screenshot becomes a `feature` slide. Screenshots are the
   scarce resource — build the carousel around them first.
2. Group the rest by platform or theme into `list` slides. Two list slides is
   plenty; a third means the post is too long.
3. Drop anything that cannot be explained in one line: internal refactors,
   "other minor interface improvements", contributor credits.
4. Seven or eight slides total. Instagram allows more; attention does not.

## Writing the lines

- **Title**: the benefit, not the mechanism. "Warnings on every route", not
  "Route warnings are now calculated for alternate routes". Under 30 characters
  where possible; it must not wrap to three lines.
- **Body**: exactly one sentence saying what changed. Assume no prior context.
- **List item**: one line, no trailing period, starts with the thing that
  changed rather than the verb.
- Second person ("you"), present tense, no exclamation marks.
- Never credit contributors on the images — that belongs in the full changelog.
- Keep the project's terminology: *bookmark*, *track*, *route*, *contour lines*,
  *OpenStreetMap* (spelled out, not OSM).

## Caption

Structure:

```
One-line hook that says what the update is about.

• four to six highlights, shortest first
• …

Get it at get.omaps.org — free, offline, ad-free and open source.

#hashtags
```

Under ~2 200 characters, since Instagram truncates the rest. Only the first
line and a half is visible before "more", so front-load it.

Hashtag set that fits the project — mix broad and niche, around 12:

```
#organicmaps #openstreetmap #offlinemaps #opensource #privacy #foss
#hiking #cycling #navigation #gps #travel #maps
```
